Corrects the pinched trousers and detached-looking boots in manual-v4.

Source: art/borin.walk-left.promoted-1790377807996.png
Rebuild: node demos/forest/scripts/animate-borin-side-walk.mjs

Each leg is one textured mesh from hip through knee into the original boot.
Mesh bands share their edges, including the boot cuff, to prevent gaps.
The hips have distinct positions beneath the tunic; both legs use the same source pixels.
A trouser overlap sits under the moving tunic hem without copying its gold trim.
The generator checks a continuous opaque path from each hip to its own boot
before layering the two legs, so the other leg cannot hide a disconnected shoe.

Original upper-body frames, color palette, 8-frame alternating step sequence,
100x140 frame cells, 10 fps playback and mirrored right walk are unchanged.
Body bob remains 0,+2,0,-2 repeated.
