Manual pixel animation, explicitly requested after reviewing generated alternatives.

Source: art/borin.walk-left.promoted-1790377807996.png
Motion reference: https://krznmedia.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/walk-cycle.gif
Rebuild: node demos/forest/scripts/animate-borin-side-walk.mjs
Inspect joints: add --joints to the rebuild command.

Eight frames: contact, recoil, passing, high point, repeated with the other leg supporting.
Near and far legs use the same original leather pixels, four frames out of phase.
Both legs have independently positioned hip, knee, ankle and boot transforms.
The far leg is drawn first; the near leg occludes it where they cross.
No palette changes, relighting, new AI generation or per-frame body scaling.
Original upper-body frames provide the counter-swinging arm poses.

Body bob in native pixels: 0, +2, 0, -2, 0, +2, 0, -2.
Helmet top by frame: 21, 23, 21, 19, 21, 23, 21, 19.
Ground contact stays at y=120; airborne feet pass above it.
The original 100x140 frame cells, 3x3 packing and playback speed are retained.
The character prefab mirrors the same sheet for walking right.
